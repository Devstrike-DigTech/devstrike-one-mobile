# One Business (owner app)

For business owners and their teams. Everything sits behind One ID sign-in; the router sends signed-out
people to `/sign-in`. After sign-in: a store switcher in the app bar, Home (empty dashboard until stores
are linked in One-1), and Inbox, Books and Insights, which state plainly when they arrive rather than
showing sample data.

See the workspace README for running, flavours and One ID client registration.
